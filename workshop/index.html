Kids, AI and You · A 45-minute session

Your child is already talking to AI. Here's what to know.

A free session for parents, carers, schools and community groups. We cover how children really use AI chatbots, where those conversations go wrong, and what you can do tonight. Led by Kate Julia, founder of Kithfold AI and two-time TEDx speaker.

“Bring your questions. All of them.”

64%of UK children aged 9 to 17 use AI chatbots.Internet Matters, Me, Myself and AI, July 2025
1 in 3children aged 11 to 16 have told a chatbot something they wouldn't tell their parents, teachers or friends.Censuswide for VodafoneThree and Childline, January 2026

What happens in the 45 minutes

  1. 0–5 min
    Why this matters nowWhat changed when chatbots started talking back, and why the usual parental controls miss it.
  2. 5–15 min
    What children are really doing with AIHomework helpers, chat companions, talking toys and voice assistants, and what keeps them coming back.
  3. 15–25 min
    Where it goes wrongKeeping secrets, “I'm your best friend”, long chats that drift, and questions about personal details. Real examples, explained plainly.
  4. 25–35 min
    What you can do tonightHow to start the conversation, the settings worth checking, signs to look for, and where to go for help.
  5. 35–45 min
    Your questionsAnything you've wondered about. Everyone leaves with a one-page guide to take home.
