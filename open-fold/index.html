Open Fold · Every quarter

Full Kithfold certification, with the fee covered.

Open Fold is for edtech teams, early-stage startups and non-profits building AI for children. Each quarter we cover certification for a small number of teams. Our founder, Kate Julia, reads every application herself.

“New here? Let's see if you qualify.”

Who can apply

  • Edtech teams building for schools or families
  • Early-stage startups building for children
  • Non-profits, charities and social enterprises

What you get

  • A full certification run against the Kithfold standard
  • Human-signed findings, with the transcripts behind them
  • The Kithfold AI Certified seal for your scope, once you pass

What we ask

  • A short case study we can publish
  • Permission to use de-identified findings to strengthen the standard
  • Holds fixed before the seal is issued
